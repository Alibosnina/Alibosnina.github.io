Logistics 2025 – 2026

Fox Line Logistics

Freight platform · Europe to Libya

A shipment leaves a European warehouse and arrives at a door in Libya. Everything in between lives here: intake, warehouse work, consolidation, routing, customs paperwork, delivery and tracking.

Underneath it sits real bookkeeping. Journal lines have to balance, months lock once they close, and every cost traces back to a warehouse or a client. You never edit a balance, you post an entry. One NestJS API feeds two Next.js apps, a staff dashboard and a customer portal, sharing types and permission codes between them.
